# Vote4U native app

Free, nonpartisan US civic-engagement app. Voting remains the primary utility: polling-place lookup with honest source labels, ZIP entry without location permission, saved locations and election reminders. The electoral map explains allocation. News stays last when enabled. Vote4U is independent of government.

The November 2026 native Local overhaul serves Portland, Oregon. Users can search the official city services directory, open real reporting/contact pages, browse upcoming and past public meetings, read recorded Council actions, and follow meetings locally. Following saves records on the phone and refreshes while using the app; it does not provide push notifications. Dates, links, and dispositions remain tied to official sources. Calendar saving requires a recent source check and a future scheduled meeting.

Owner direction: “less boring, but not too complicated.” Keep native iPhone navigation, readable system type, the established blue accent, and a clear action hierarchy. Apply Humanizer to explain actions directly without marketing slogans or repeated caveats. See docs/local-overhaul/BRIEF.md for the implementation contract and DATA.md for feed boundaries.

This checkout contains the native extension, not the separate web redesign. No new visual identity or image comp was approved. iOS 17 is the minimum; native accessibility and official-source truth take precedence over generic design defaults.
